% Aim, method, main result with its uncertainty, and what it means.
\begin{abstract}
This report measures the gravitational acceleration from the period of a simple pendulum timed at nine lengths between \qtylist{0.2;1.0}{\metre}. A least-squares fit of the small-angle period gives $g = \qty{9.809(4)}{\metre\per\second\squared}$, within one standard error of the local value, and the residuals show no trend with length.
\end{abstract}
