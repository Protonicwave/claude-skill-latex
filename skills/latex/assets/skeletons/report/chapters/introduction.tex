\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

% The question, why it matters, and how the report answers it.
A mass on a light string of length $L$ swings with a period that depends only on $L$ and the gravitational acceleration $g$ when the amplitude is small~\cite{nelson1986}. Timing the period at several lengths therefore measures $g$, and the spread of the measurements about the model tests whether the small-angle approximation holds. \Cref{ch:method} sets out the model and the measurement, \cref{ch:results} gives the results and \cref{ch:conclusions} draws the conclusions.
