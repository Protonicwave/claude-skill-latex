\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}

% What was found, how far to trust it, and what would test it further.
The simple pendulum gives $g = \qty{9.809(4)}{\metre\per\second\squared}$, and the small-angle model describes its period at every length measured. A constant error in length, such as a misjudged centre of the bob, changes the shape of the curve only slightly over this range, so the residuals test it weakly; measuring to the top and the bottom of the bob would bound it.
