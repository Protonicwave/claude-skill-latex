% What was found, how far to trust it, and what would test it further.
The simple pendulum gives $g = \qty{9.809(4)}{\metre\per\second\squared}$, and the small-angle model describes its period at every length measured. Measuring the length to the top and to the bottom of the bob would bound a constant error in length, which the residuals test only weakly.
